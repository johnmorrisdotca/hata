# Flag audit, 20 October 2026

A read-only audit of every flag in Hata 1.6.1 (466 codes, 432 distinct pictures, 905 SVG files counting frames and
variants). When it was written nothing was fixed except Manitoba (`CA-MB`, added in 1.6.1). Each finding says how it was
measured, and what was only a heuristic. The proposed fixes are at the end, in small releases.

**Status.** The findings of releases 1.6.2 (correctness) and 1.6.3 (data refresh) are fixed: each is marked **Fixed in
1.6.2** or **Fixed in 1.6.3** where it is made below, and the list of proposed fixes at the end says what is still
open (1.6.4 onward).

## 1. Missing flags

Measured against ISO 3166-1 and ISO 3166-2 as shipped in the `pycountry` package (the Debian iso-codes data, read on the
audit day) and against Kuni's lists, which Hata's coverage rule follows.

**Countries.** ISO 3166-1 has 249 codes; Kuni adds `XK` (Kosovo), so 250. Every one has a flag or a stated reason: 245
have a flag, and 5 are left out with reasons in `docs/left-out.md` (`AQ`, `BQ`, `EH` no flag of their own to ship;
`CC` and `OM` licence). No ISO code is missing from Kuni. Hata documents no special codes (`EU`, `UN`, the reserved
`UK` and so on), so none is offered.

**Subdivisions of the ten countries Hata covers.** Kuni's first level equals ISO 3166-2's first level in every case,
code for code (no code on one side only):

Total: 233 of 233 first-level codes accounted for, 221 with a flag and 12 left out with a reason.

**Subdivision sets Hata does not cover.** 200 countries have ISO 3166-2 first-level codes (3,590 in all); Hata

Mexico and India wait (CC BY-SA drawings, no official flags).

## 2. Format and safety

All 905 files in `dist/svg/` were checked by pattern and parsed as XML.

- Every file is an `<svg>` root, well formed, with a `viewBox` and the SVG namespace.
- No `<script>`, event handler, `<foreignObject>`, `<iframe>`, `<object>`, `<embed>` or animation element.
- No external reference: no `href`/`src` that does not start with `#`, no `url()` that is not a local `#id`, no
  `@import`, no DTD or entity.
- **No raster picture**: no `<image>` and no `data:image` anywhere. The tarball check in `scripts/check-package.mjs`
  also refuses one.
- No `<text>` and no `<style>` element in any shipped file (no font dependence).
- 9 flags use gradients (`US-NY`, `EC`, `MX`, `GS`, `GT`, `NI`, `US-VT`, `VG`, `US-LA`) and 54 use a mask, clip path or
  filter. All are internal and inline-safe. Ids are prefixed per flag (`hata-<code>-`), so two flags in one page cannot
  collide.
- 394 codes carry Commons' non-copyright restriction `insignia` (national emblems and flags may be limited by law), 9
  carry `communist` (`AO`, `BF`, `CN`, `CU`, `CZ`, `KP`, `LA`, `MZ`, `VN`), 3 `noresize` (`IR`, `PS`, `SA`), and `CA`
  `trademarked`, `IL` `israelflag`. They are in each flag's manifest record; the README and NOTICE already say they are
  Commons' words.

## 3. Size

- Total 8,539 KB raw, 2,990 KB gzipped. Median 760 B, 90th percentile 65 KB, 95th percentile 115 KB, maximum 372 KB.
- Over 10 KB: 109 pictures (95% of the bytes). Over 20 KB: 79 (90%). Over 40 KB, Hata's own budget: 55 (82%). Over 80
  KB: 36 (69%).
- The flags that are long-detail illustrations (state seals and coats of arms) are nearly all the weight.

**What optimisation could still save.** Shipped files already went through SVGO 4 with a per-flag measured precision. Each
of the 79 pictures over 20 KB was run again through SVGO with precision 2, 1 and 0, with and without `reusePaths`, and
drawn in Chromium at 960 px wide against the shipped picture. Taking the smallest result that differs in no more than
0.1% of pixels: **152 KB raw (2.0% of 7,694 KB) and 46 KB gzipped, on 17 of the 79 flags**, the rest saving nothing. The
biggest wins are `US-SD` (200 to 148 KB) and `US-OR` (161 to 119 KB); the ten largest flags gain nothing. So lossless
optimisation is spent. A real saving needs simplified drawings of the heaviest seals offered beside the full ones
(already on the README's roadmap), or loading them lazily.

## 4. Quality

**Proportions.** The distribution of the 466 codes is as expected: 194 at 3:2, 97 at 2:1, 46 at 5:3, 25 at 7:10, 23 square
(Switzerland, the Vatican and the cantons), and the known odd ones at their official ratios (Nepal 0.82, Belgium 13:15,
Denmark 28:37, Norway 8:11, Sweden 5:8, Finland 11:18, Estonia 7:11, Mexico and Iran 4:7, Bolivia 15:22, Paraguay 11:20,
Guam and the United States 10:19, Monaco 4:5, Cape Verde 10:17). One was found wrong, and one needs checking:

- **Fixed in 1.6.2.** **Qatar (`QA`) is drawn at 25:6 (4.17:1); its official ratio is 11:28 (about 2.55:1).** Commons' file is
  `width="1400" height="550" viewBox="0 0 75 18" preserveAspectRatio="none"`: the drawing is meant to be stretched to
  28:11. Hata keeps the `viewBox` and removes `preserveAspectRatio`, so the flag is about 64% too wide and the scallops
  are flattened. It is the only one of the 449 Commons source files with `preserveAspectRatio="none"`, and the only
  one whose `viewBox` ratio disagrees with its `width` and `height` (a scan flagged three more, Espírito Santo, Berlin and
  Brandenburg, but they give their size in mm, and ship at their true 7:10 and 5:3). `docs/provenance.md` repeats "4.17:1" as if it were true to the flag. 1.6.2 ships Qatar at 28:11 and a test holds every
  flag to its source's shape.
- Prince Edward Island (`CA-PE`) is 1751:1200 (about 1.46:1). Its official ratio was not confirmed here, so
  it is a question, not a finding. **Fixed in 1.6.2:** the Provincial Flag Act's Schedule gives 3:2 (six feet in the fly,
  four in the hoist), recorded with its source in `scripts/official-ratios.data.json`, and the flag ships at 3:2.

**Colours.** No official specification was fetched per country, so this is a spot check, not a full audit: 17 flags with
a published specification were read from the shipped SVG and agree with the published specification as the auditor knows it, without the specifications being fetched (Canada `#d52b1e`, Japan `#bc002d`, the United
Kingdom `#012169` / `#c8102e`, Germany `#dd0000` / `#ffce00`, France `#002654` / `#ce1126` (the post-2020 blue), Italy
`#009246` / `#ce2b37`, Sweden `#005293` / `#fecb00`, the Netherlands `#ae1c28` / `#21468b`, Ireland `#169b62` / `#ff883e`,
Korea `#cd2e3a` / `#0047a0`, the United States `#bf0a30` / `#00205b` (the Pantone drawing), and others). `docs/compared.md`
already lists the 51 places where another flag set draws something different, every one reviewed.

**Outdated designs.** Checked against the Commons upload date and the colours drawn: current for the ones that changed
recently: Syria (the 2025 flag is the default, the Assad-era one a variant), Kyrgyzstan (2023), Mississippi (2021, the
magnolia flag), Utah (2024) and Minnesota (2024): upload dates after the change, and colours that match the new designs. Kazakhstan's flag has not changed (its Commons file was last uploaded 2023-04-24). Mauritania and Malawi were
judged by upload date only (2023-07 and 2026-08), not by looking. Afghanistan deliberately ships the Islamic Republic's
flag as the default (decision of 2026-10-09). Nothing outdated found.

**Detail.** Flags whose emblem is drawn in 2 KB or less are likely simplified versions, which need a person to look
(heuristic, byte size only): `KE` 1.0 KB, `SK` 1.2 KB, `AO` 1.6 KB, `ZW` 1.8 KB, `MZ` 1.9 KB, `MN` 1.2 KB, `IR` 1.3 KB,
`UZ` 1.3 KB. Peru, Bolivia and Costa Rica (168 to 213 B) appear to ship the civil flag without arms, which is a choice, not a defect.
No raster picture is embedded anywhere. Whether any vector was auto-traced from a raster was not judged; the most
path-heavy flags (`SV`, `CA-PE`, `ES`, `US-MO`, `US-NY`, `US-WV`, `EC`) are the ones to look at first.

**Source, author and licence metadata.** Every flag has a Commons page, a licence kind and an upload date, except `YE`,
the one MIT drawing from country-flag-icons (its notice is in NOTICE.md). Licences: 456 public domain, 8 CC0, 1 MIT,
1 accepted by decision (Mississippi). **Fixed in 1.6.3: 38 flags have no author in their record** (Commons' Artist field is empty or
absent): `CK FM GN GS GW JE KW MG MP MS NF TM TZ AT-1 AT-2 AT-5 AT-8 AT-9 BR-GO BR-PA DE-BB DE-HB DE-HE DE-MV DE-NI
DE-SH DE-SL DE-ST DE-TH US-AR US-GA US-IA US-ME US-MI US-MP US-NC US-NM US-WI`. Their licence is still stated. 1.6.3 gives all 38 an author: Schleswig-Holstein's now comes from Commons itself (the
drawing was re-uploaded with an author field), and the other 37 are read from each file's description page, which names
a drawer, an artist or a flag collection for 22 of them and nobody for the other 15 ("Unknown author", Commons' own
words, with a source or a later editor added in brackets where the page gives one), never a name taken from the file
history (`scripts/authors.data.json`, `docs/decisions.md`, "Authors Commons does not name"). Two variant drawings
(Guadeloupe's local flag and Wallis and Futuna's) that the count did not include were empty the same way and are filled
too. A test fails for a flag with no author, and for an entry that Commons has since overtaken.

**Other findings.**

- **Fixed in 1.6.3.** The Commons snapshot is dated 2026-10-09. Re-reading it on 2026-10-20 shows 15 files whose Commons metadata or
  templates changed (British Columbia, Burkina Faso, Christmas Island, Iwate, Kanagawa, Russia, Saitama, Shiga,
  Tochigi, Uzbekistan, Yamagata, Yamaguchi, the Philippines, the UAE) and one whose drawing changed: Schleswig-Holstein
  (362 B to 232 B, a simplification of the same flag, re-uploaded 2026-10-11). Not refreshed here, so Manitoba's entry
  is the only one in the snapshot read on a later day. 1.6.3 takes the snapshot of 2026-10-20: of the 449 files, 448 are
  byte for byte the ones kept (same SHA-1) and Schleswig-Holstein's new drawing is accepted, being the same flag (blue,
  white and red stripes at 5:3) and still public domain under `PD-Flag-Germany`, its two small colour changes (blue
  `#0039ad` to `#0f47af`, red `#d61810` to `#da121a`) invisible at a glance; the other changes are Commons' own metadata
  and nothing that bears on a licence (see the changelog of 1.6.3).
- **Fixed in 1.6.2.** README's "Subdivisions" row says the United States is "56 of 57"; the same README and the build say 57 of 57.

## Proposed fixes, in small releases

1. **1.6.2, correctness (done, 2026-10-20).** Qatar: ship the flag at 28:11 (honour `preserveAspectRatio="none"` by reading `width` and
   `height`), update its `docs/provenance.md` line and add a test that a shipped flag's ratio equals its Commons
   `width:height` unless a reason is recorded. Check Prince Edward Island's ratio. Fix the README's "56 of 57".
2. **1.6.3, data refresh (done, 2026-10-20).** Re-run `pnpm data:commons` and `pnpm data:tune`, review the 15 changed files and
   Schleswig-Holstein's new drawing, rebuild. Fill the 38 missing authors from the Commons file history, or record
   "author not stated" explicitly.
3. **1.6.4, detail review.** Look at `KE SK AO ZW MZ MN IR UZ` beside their official designs and note any that are
   simplified; run a colour check against each flag's Wikipedia specification table.
4. **1.7.0, optional simplified seals.** Offer simplified variants of the 20 heaviest flags (under 20 KB each) beside the
   full ones; drop the lossless re-optimisation (worth 2%).
5. **Later minor releases, one country each** (a suggested order, free drawings and an official flag first): the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Argentina, South Africa; Spain, Italy and Mexico stay
   blocked on their decisions.
